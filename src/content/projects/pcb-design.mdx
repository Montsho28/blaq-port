### Overview
Created a fully functioning PCB to toggle two lights on and off using a 555 timer IC and a 7473 flip-flop IC. The PCB was designed using Proteus, and the components were soldered onto the board to create a compact and efficient circuit. The 555 timer IC was used to generate a clock signal, which was then fed into the 7473 flip-flop IC to toggle the state of the lights. The PCB design was optimized for minimal space usage while ensuring proper functionality. This project allowed me to gain hands-on experience in PCB design, soldering, and working with integrated circuits, enhancing my skills in electronics and hardware development.
